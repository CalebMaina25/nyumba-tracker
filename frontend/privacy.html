Privacy Policy - NyumbaTracker

Last Updated: 16 Oct 2026

NyumbaTracker ("we", "app") helps landlords manage tenants and rent. We respect your privacy.

1. Data We Collect

  • Phone number and password you provide during registration
  • Tenant names, houses, rent amounts you enter (stored locally on your device)
  • We do NOT collect location, contacts, or photos

2. How Data is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). Data is not sent to our servers except when you use online sync. We do not sell data.

3. Data Deletion

You can delete all data by clearing app storage in Settings > Apps > NyumbaTracker > Clear Data or using Logout > Clear Data button in app.

4. Contact

Email: support@nyumbatracker.co.ke
